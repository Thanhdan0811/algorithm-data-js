## How to use this note

1. **Watch first** with the script closed — train your listening.
2. Open the script (toggle below) and read along on a second watch.
3. Study the **Vocabulary**, **Phrases & Idioms**, and **Grammar** sections.
4. Pick 5–10 items, then **shadow** (repeat out loud after the speaker).

## 💬 Phrases, Idioms & Slang

> Each entry has: **📖 Meaning** (a full sentence) · **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt** · **📝 Examples**
> (🎬 = line from the video · ✏️ = an extra example to practice with).

### 1. to fill out (a form)
- **📖 Meaning:** To write all the required information into the blank spaces of a form.
- **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt:** điền (thông tin) vào đơn/phiếu.
- **📝 Examples:**
  - 🎬 "I'm going to **fill out** this form and then hand it back."
  - ✏️ "Please **fill out** your name and address here."

### 2. are we supposed to...?
- **📖 Meaning:** A way to ask whether something is expected, required, or the correct thing to do.
- **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt:** chúng ta có phải / có nên... không? (hỏi về quy định, điều được mong đợi).
- **📝 Examples:**
  - 🎬 "Are we **supposed to** wash our face?"
  - ✏️ "Am I **supposed to** wear a mask inside?"

### 3. so far
- **📖 Meaning:** Up to this moment in time (the situation might still change later).
- **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt:** cho đến giờ / tính đến lúc này.
- **📝 Examples:**
  - 🎬 "**So far** I really like the vibe of this place."
  - ✏️ "The trip has been great **so far**."

### 4. all the way
- **📖 Meaning:** For the entire distance, or completely — from start to finish.
- **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt:** suốt cả quãng đường / hết cỡ.
- **📝 Examples:**
  - 🎬 "We came from Hongdae **all the way** to Gangnam Station."
  - ✏️ "He drove **all the way** home without stopping."

### 5. you're cooked *(slang)*
- **📖 Meaning:** You are in serious trouble, or there's no hope for you — often said jokingly.
- **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt:** (lóng) tiêu rồi / xong đời rồi / hết cứu.
- **📝 Examples:**
  - 🎬 "You old. **You're cooked**. By the time you're 40, you'll look 50."
  - ✏️ "If the teacher sees this, **we're cooked**."

### 6. by the time...
- **📖 Meaning:** When a certain future moment arrives / before that moment happens.
- **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt:** đến lúc mà... / trước khi...
- **📝 Examples:**
  - 🎬 "**By the time** you're like 40, you're going to look 50."
  - ✏️ "**By the time** we arrived, the show had already started."

### 7. that's just the nature of...
- **📖 Meaning:** That is simply how something is — it can't really be changed.
- **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt:** đó là bản chất của... / vốn dĩ là vậy.
- **📝 Examples:**
  - 🎬 "But that's just **the nature of** being a man."
  - ✏️ "Stress is just **the nature of** this job."

### 8. to go with (an option)
- **📖 Meaning:** To choose or decide on a particular option.
- **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt:** chọn / quyết định theo (phương án nào đó).
- **📝 Examples:**
  - 🎬 "They suggested some treatments that we're going to **go with**."
  - ✏️ "I think I'll **go with** the blue one."

### 9. to put someone on (something)
- **📖 Meaning:** To introduce someone to something good so they start using or enjoying it.
- **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt:** giới thiệu / "mách" cho ai đó biết đến, bắt đầu dùng cái gì.
- **📝 Examples:**
  - 🎬 "I kind of **put you on** that like five years ago."
  - ✏️ "My friend **put me on** this new coffee shop."

### 10. to back up
- **📖 Meaning:** To move a vehicle backwards / to reverse.
- **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt:** lùi (xe) / de xe.
- **📝 Examples:**
  - 🎬 "This truck is **backing up** into this building."
  - ✏️ "**Back up** a little — you're too close to the wall."

- **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt:** đang thi công / đang xây dựng, sửa chữa.

  - 🎬 "They can speak English to you. So **it's a win-win**."
  - ✏️ "You learn, I get help — **it's a win-win**."

### 17. that's all that matters
- **📖 Meaning:** That is the only important thing; nothing else needs to be perfect.
- **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt:** chỉ cần vậy là đủ / đó mới là điều quan trọng nhất.
- **📝 Examples:**
  - 🎬 "Yeah, I will try. — **That's all that matters**."
  - ✏️ "You tried your best, and **that's all that matters**."

### 18. freeze to death *(hyperbole)*
- **📖 Meaning:** To be extremely cold (an exaggeration, not literal).
- **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt:** lạnh chết cóng / lạnh muốn chết.
- **📝 Examples:**
  - 🎬 "In winter it's minus 40 in Canada. You'll **freeze to death**."
  - ✏️ "Bring a jacket or you'll **freeze to death** out there."

### 19. to end up (doing something)
- **📖 Meaning:** To finally be in a certain situation or do something, often unexpectedly.
- **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt:** rốt cuộc / cuối cùng lại (làm gì, thành ra sao).
- **📝 Examples:**
  - 🎬 "I thought they'd stay, but then they **ended up leaving**."
  - ✏️ "We **ended up** watching movies all night."

### 20. to come up (to someone)
- **📖 Meaning:** To approach someone, usually to talk to them.
- **🇻🇳 Tiếng Việt:** tiến đến / lại gần (bắt chuyện với ai).
- **📝 Examples:**
  - 🎬 "That was the first time someone has **come up** and spoke to me."
  - ✏️ "A stranger **came up to** me and asked for directions."

## 🔧 Grammar & Sentence Structures

> Each point has: the rule · **🇻🇳** a Vietnamese explanation · 🎬 lines from the video · ✏️ an extra example.

### 1. Future with *going to* (plans & predictions)
Form: **am/is/are + going to + [verb]**. Used for plans you've decided on and predictions based on what you see now.
- **🇻🇳** Dùng *going to* cho dự định đã quyết hoặc dự đoán dựa trên bằng chứng hiện tại. *"gonna"* là cách nói tắt của *going to*.
- 🎬 "I**'m going to** fill out this form." *(plan / dự định)*
- 🎬 "What do you think your skin **is going to be** like?" *(prediction / dự đoán)*
- 🎬 "Robots **are gonna** replace you." *(gonna = casual "going to")*
- ✏️ "It's cloudy — it**'s going to** rain."

### 2. Present continuous for narration / right now
Form: **am/is/are + [verb]-ing**. Describes an action happening at this moment — perfect for vlogging.
- **🇻🇳** Thì hiện tại tiếp diễn: hành động đang xảy ra ngay lúc nói (rất hợp để tường thuật khi quay video).
- 🎬 "Right now we**'re** in the dressing room."
- 🎬 "This truck **is backing up** into this building."
- ✏️ "I**'m walking** to the station right now."

### 3. Present perfect (experience & unfinished time)
Form: **have/has + [past participle]**. Connects the past to now — life experiences, or time still continuing.
- **🇻🇳** Thì hiện tại hoàn thành: nối quá khứ với hiện tại — kinh nghiệm sống, hoặc khoảng thời gian còn tiếp diễn.
- 🎬 "I**'ve never done** a skin test **before**." *(life experience / kinh nghiệm)*
- 🎬 "I**'ve been learning** English **for** three months." *(present perfect continuous — bắt đầu trong quá khứ, vẫn tiếp diễn)*
- 🎬 "**Have you** ever **been** camping?" *(experience question / hỏi kinh nghiệm)*
- 🎬 "I**'ve been** in Korea 26 days." *(duration up to now / thời lượng đến giờ)*
- ✏️ "I**'ve visited** Japan three times."

> **for** + a length of time (*for three months* — trong 3 tháng) vs. **since** + a start point (*since March* — từ tháng Ba).

### 4. *be supposed to* (expectation / rule)
Form: **am/is/are + supposed to + [verb]**. Talks about what is expected, required, or the correct thing to do.
- **🇻🇳** *be supposed to* = điều được cho là phải/nên làm (theo quy định hoặc mong đợi).
- 🎬 "Are we **supposed to** wash our face?" = Is that what we're expected to do?
- ✏️ "You**'re supposed to** turn off your phone in the cinema."

### 5. Comparatives & superlatives
Comparative (**-er / more … than**) compares two things; superlative (**the -est / the most**) picks out the top one.
- **🇻🇳** So sánh hơn (*-er/more … than*) so sánh 2 vật; so sánh nhất (*the -est/the most*) chỉ ra cái nhất.
- 🎬 "his skin is five years **older than** he is" *(comparative + "than")*
- 🎬 "mine**'s worse**" *(irregular / bất quy tắc: bad → worse)*
- 🎬 "the **most** beautiful part of Canada" *(superlative)*
- 🎬 "the **most** important part" · "**most** importantly"
- ✏️ "Vancouver is **more beautiful than** Toronto." / "It's **the best** city."

### 6. *would* for opinions & polite/hypothetical statements
**would + [verb]** softens an opinion or describes an imagined/hypothetical situation.
- **🇻🇳** *would* làm câu nói nhẹ nhàng, lịch sự hơn, hoặc diễn tả tình huống giả định.
- 🎬 "I **would recommend** this place." *(soft opinion / ý kiến nhẹ nhàng)*
- 🎬 "you **would call** this a harness" *(general truth / cách gọi chung)*
- 🎬 "This **would be** a cool concept." *(hypothetical / giả định)*
- ✏️ "I **would love** to visit Canada someday."

### 7. First conditional (real future possibility)
Form: **If + present simple, … will / [present result]**. For real, likely future situations.
- **🇻🇳** Câu điều kiện loại 1: điều kiện có thật, có khả năng xảy ra trong tương lai (If + hiện tại đơn, … will…).
- 🎬 "**If** you go there **and** speak to foreigners, **it's** really easy to make friends."
- 🎬 "**If** you **ever do see** me, come speak to me." *(do = emphasis / nhấn mạnh)*
- ✏️ "**If** you practice every day, you **will** improve."

### 8. *once* + present, for "as soon as"
**once + [present simple]** = "as soon as / after" something happens.
- **🇻🇳** *once* + hiện tại đơn = "ngay khi / sau khi" điều gì đó xảy ra.
- 🎬 "**Once** I started speaking, I started learning exponentially."
- 🎬 "**Once** self-driving vehicles get good enough, this would be cool."
- ✏️ "**Once** you finish, let me know."

### 9. Filler words & natural speech (listen for these!)
Native casual speech is full of these — learn to *hear past* them.
- **🇻🇳** Từ đệm trong lời nói tự nhiên — hầu như không mang nghĩa, chỉ để câu giờ suy nghĩ và làm câu nói mềm hơn. Đừng dịch sát nghĩa.
- **like** (filler / từ đệm): "This is **like** the dressing room." / "It's **like** really clean."
- **kind of / kinda** (hơi hơi): "That's actually **kind of** cool."
- **I guess** (chắc là / tôi đoán): "**I guess** this is an electric vehicle."
- **stuff / and all this stuff** (mấy thứ đó, vân vân): "wash your face **and stuff**."
- **dude / guys** (cách gọi thân mật: ông/mày, các cậu): casual address.

> These carry little meaning — they buy thinking time and soften statements. Don't translate them literally.
